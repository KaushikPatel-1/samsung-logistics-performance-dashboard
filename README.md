Samsung Supply Chain & Logistics Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI Data Analytics Project focused on analysing Samsung’s supply chain and logistics performance. This project explores business performance, supplier efficiency, inventory management, shipment activity, delivery delays and procurement costs through data-driven visualisations.

⸻

Project Overview

Supply chain management plays an important role in business operations. Efficient supplier management, inventory control and timely deliveries help businesses improve operational performance and manage costs.

The objective of this project is to analyse Samsung’s supply chain data and transform it into meaningful business insights using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard is divided into two main analytical pages:

1. Executive Overview
2. Supplier Analysis

⸻

Project Objectives

* Analyse overall business performance using key performance indicators (KPIs).
* Monitor revenue, profit and order quantity.
* Understand inventory and shipment performance.
* Analyse supplier lead time and procurement costs.
* Compare delivery delays across shipping carriers.
* Identify monthly cost trends and variations.
* Generate data-driven insights to support business decisions.

Key Insights Observed

Based on the current dashboard:

* December records the highest displayed monthly total unit cost at approximately 8.01M.
* September records the lowest displayed monthly total unit cost at approximately 5.01M.
* China has the highest displayed average lead time at 12.5.
* Vietnam has the lowest displayed average lead time at 9.4.
* Maersk Line has the highest displayed carrier delay count at 87.
* Amazon.com Inc. generates the highest displayed revenue among the platforms, at approximately 37M.

These observations highlight areas that may require further investigation.

Business Problems Addressed

This project focuses on analysing important supply chain challenges:

* Delivery delays and logistics performance.
* Supplier lead-time differences.
* Procurement and unit-cost variations.
* Inventory management and stock availability.
* Shipment and order performance.
* Monthly cost fluctuations.

⸻

Recommendations

Based on the patterns observed in the dashboard, the following areas can be investigated:

1. Delivery Performance: Monitor carrier delay rates and compare them with total shipment volumes.
2. Supplier Management: Review suppliers with higher average lead times.
3. Cost Optimisation: Investigate monthly increases in total unit costs.
4. Inventory Management: Monitor stock levels to avoid shortages and excess inventory.
5. Procurement Analysis: Compare supplier costs using consistent and clearly defined calculations.

⸻

Conclusion

This project demonstrates how data analytics and Power BI can help understand supply chain and logistics operations.

By analysing business KPIs, supplier performance, inventory levels, shipment delays and procurement costs, the dashboard provides a consolidated view of operational performance and highlights opportunities for further investigation and improvement.
